# Signal Blue Light

A bright, modern blue theme anchored by `#366AFF`, built for product strategy, technology storytelling, and executive update decks that want the confidence of Signal Blue without a dark canvas.

- **Primary Accent**: `#366AFF` - confident system blue for focal emphasis, numbered stages, and strong calls to action
- **Secondary Accent**: `#59C3FF` - fresh cyan-blue lift for gradients, atmospheric glow, and motion-friendly highlights
- **Canvas Base**: `#F7FAFF` - clean light background with enough cool tint to feel digital rather than generic white
- **Surface Primary**: `#FFFFFF` - crisp card and panel surface for readable layered layouts
- **Surface Secondary**: `#DCE8FF` - supportive blue-tinted surface for diagrams, grouped content, and secondary structure
- **Primary Text**: `#18324A` - dark blue-gray for high legibility and a less harsh feel than pure black
- **Secondary Text**: `#5B6F89` - muted supporting text that stays calm against the bright background

Best for:
- product strategy and planning decks
- technology capability overviews
- modern consulting and transformation presentations
- executive updates that need a lighter, more open tone
