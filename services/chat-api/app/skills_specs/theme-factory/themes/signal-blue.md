# Signal Blue

A sharp, modern blue theme anchored by `#366AFF`, built for product strategy, technology transformation, and executive update decks that need confidence, clarity, and a distinctly digital tone without falling into generic navy corporate styling.

- **Primary Accent**: `#366AFF` - confident system blue for focal emphasis, highlights, and action surfaces
- **Secondary Accent**: `#59C3FF` - bright cyan-blue lift for gradients, glow treatments, and visual energy
- **Canvas Base**: `#08111F` - deep blue-black background that keeps the theme crisp and high-contrast
- **Surface Primary**: `#12233D` - core card and panel surface with enough depth for layered dark layouts
- **Surface Secondary**: `#193257` - elevated structure surface for diagrams, secondary cards, and grouped content
- **Primary Text**: `#F5F9FF` - clean high-contrast text for titles and key labels
- **Secondary Text**: `#C5D3EB` - softer blue-gray text for supporting copy and annotations

Best for:
- AI and platform strategy decks
- enterprise technology planning
- product architecture and roadmap presentations
- modern consulting and transformation narratives
