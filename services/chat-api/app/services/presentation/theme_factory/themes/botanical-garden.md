# Botanical Garden

A fresh and organic theme featuring vibrant garden-inspired colors for lively presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Fern Green**: `#4a7c59` - Rich natural green
- **Marigold**: `#f9a620` - Bright floral accent
- **Terracotta**: `#b7472a` - Earthy warm tone
- **Cream**: `#f5f3ed` - Soft neutral backgrounds

## Color Roles (Recommended)

- **Primary visual anchor**: Use **Fern Green** (`#4a7c59`) for section headings, key containers, and major emphasis.
- **Secondary highlight**: Use **Marigold** (`#f9a620`) for key data points, icons, and callouts.
- **Warm contrast accent**: Use **Terracotta** (`#b7472a`) sparingly for alerts, contrast cards, or emphasis labels.
- **Content surface**: Use **Cream** (`#f5f3ed`) or white for body content zones.

## Practical Usage Rules

- Keep each page balanced: **green-led base + one warm accent + neutral surface**.
- Use marigold and terracotta sparingly; both are strong colors.
- Ensure text contrast stays high on cream surfaces.
- Prefer rounded rectangles for process cards and scenario blocks.
- Keep connectors simple and thin (`1-2px`), with color weight lower than content blocks.

## Suggested Layout Color Mapping

- **Cover / hero page**: fern-green anchor with marigold accent points.
- **Agenda / timeline page**: cream surface, green step cards, marigold current-step emphasis.
- **Architecture / framework page**: cream/white modules, green headers, terracotta only for critical contrasts.
- **Data / chart page**: green primary series, marigold secondary highlights, terracotta for exceptions.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** keep the palette natural and structured.
- **Do** use warm accents to guide attention, not to fill large areas.
- **Don't** place marigold and terracotta in equal large blocks on one slide.
- **Don't** let decorative earthy tones overpower readability.

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Serif Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Garden centers, food presentations, farm-to-table content, botanical brands, natural products.
