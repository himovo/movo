# Desert Rose

A soft and sophisticated theme with dusty, muted tones perfect for elegant presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Dusty Rose**: `#d4a5a5` - Soft primary color
- **Clay**: `#b87d6d` - Earthy accent
- **Sand**: `#e8d5c4` - Warm neutral backgrounds
- **Deep Burgundy**: `#5d2e46` - Rich dark contrast

## Color Roles (Recommended)

- **Primary visual anchor**: Use **Deep Burgundy** (`#5d2e46`) for major title bands and strong contrast panels.
- **Secondary structure color**: Use **Clay** (`#b87d6d`) for section headers and medium-emphasis cards.
- **Soft highlight tone**: Use **Dusty Rose** (`#d4a5a5`) for key-point callouts and gentle visual accents.
- **Content surface**: Use **Sand** (`#e8d5c4`) or white for readable text regions.

## Practical Usage Rules

- Keep page composition restrained: **one dark anchor + one soft accent + neutral surface**.
- Avoid using dusty rose and clay as large simultaneous background fills.
- Maintain high contrast for body text on warm neutral backgrounds.
- Prefer rounded rectangles and clean card systems over decorative curves.
- Keep connector lines thin (`1-2px`) and subordinate to text hierarchy.

## Suggested Layout Color Mapping

- **Cover / hero page**: burgundy hero region with clay accents.
- **Agenda / timeline page**: sand/white base, clay step cards, dusty-rose current-step highlight.
- **Architecture / framework page**: warm neutral cards, burgundy titles, clay dividers.
- **Data / chart page**: clay primary bars, dusty-rose secondary bars, burgundy for key baselines.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** preserve elegance with moderate contrast and clear hierarchy.
- **Do** use burgundy for decisive emphasis.
- **Don't** over-tint entire slides in dusty rose.
- **Don't** combine many saturated warm blocks in one view.

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSans Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Fashion presentations, beauty brands, wedding planning, interior design, boutique businesses.
