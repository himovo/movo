# Golden Hour

A rich and warm autumnal palette that creates an inviting and sophisticated atmosphere.

## Color Palette

- **Mustard Yellow**: `#f4a900` - Bold primary accent
- **Terracotta**: `#c1666b` - Warm secondary color
- **Warm Beige**: `#d4b896` - Neutral backgrounds
- **Chocolate Brown**: `#4a403a` - Dark text and anchors

## Color Roles (Recommended)

- **Primary visual anchor**: Use **Mustard Yellow** (`#f4a900`) for focal highlights and major emphasis labels.
- **Secondary structure color**: Use **Terracotta** (`#c1666b`) for section headers and medium-emphasis cards.
- **Dark contrast base**: Use **Chocolate Brown** (`#4a403a`) for hero regions and strong text anchors.
- **Content surface**: Use **Warm Beige** (`#d4b896`) or white for body content zones.

## Practical Usage Rules

- Keep balance: **dark/neutral base + one warm primary accent + one support color**.
- Mustard is high energy; reserve it for key points, not full-page fills.
- Maintain readability with dark text on beige and white surfaces.
- Prefer rounded rectangular cards for process blocks and modules.
- Keep connector lines light and thin (`1-2px`) with lower visual weight than titles.

## Suggested Layout Color Mapping

- **Cover / hero page**: chocolate-brown base with mustard accents.
- **Agenda / timeline page**: neutral light surface, terracotta nodes, mustard current-step cue.
- **Architecture / framework page**: beige/white cards, terracotta titles, mustard micro-highlights.
- **Data / chart page**: terracotta primary series, mustard emphasis bars, brown baselines.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** use warm contrast to guide reading order.
- **Do** keep one dominant warm accent per section.
- **Don't** flood large areas with mustard yellow.
- **Don't** combine many saturated warm colors in dense layouts.

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSans Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Restaurant presentations, hospitality brands, fall campaigns, cozy lifestyle content, artisan products.
