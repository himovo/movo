# Sunset Boulevard

A warm and vibrant theme inspired by golden hour sunsets, perfect for energetic and creative presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Burnt Orange**: `#e76f51` - Primary accent color
- **Coral**: `#f4a261` - Secondary warm accent
- **Warm Sand**: `#e9c46a` - Highlighting and backgrounds
- **Deep Purple**: `#264653` - Dark contrast and text

## Color Roles (Recommended)

- **Primary visual anchor**: Use **Burnt Orange** (`#e76f51`) for key titles and dominant emphasis blocks.
- **Secondary accent**: Use **Coral** (`#f4a261`) for callouts, icons, and medium-priority highlights.
- **Dark contrast base**: Use **Deep Purple** (`#264653`) for hero zones and high-contrast sections.
- **Support surface/highlight**: Use **Warm Sand** (`#e9c46a`) in small or medium areas for warmth and separation.

## Practical Usage Rules

- Keep pages controlled: **one dark base + one warm primary + one support warm tone**.
- Burnt orange and coral are both vivid; avoid full-page simultaneous saturation.
- Use warm sand as support background, not as dominant full-page fill.
- Prefer rounded rectangles for cards and process steps.
- Keep connectors clean and thin (`1-2px`) so warm accents remain focal.

## Suggested Layout Color Mapping

- **Cover / hero page**: deep-purple base with burnt-orange highlights.
- **Agenda / timeline page**: white/light surface, burnt-orange nodes, coral current-step emphasis.
- **Architecture / framework page**: white cards, deep-purple headings, warm-sand separators.
- **Data / chart page**: burnt-orange primary series, coral secondary, deep-purple axis anchors.

## Do / Don't

- **Do** keep warm energy with a clear reading hierarchy.
- **Do** reserve deep purple for strong contrast anchors.
- **Don't** flood large content panels with coral/sand.
- **Don't** use too many bright warm accents in the same cluster.

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Serif Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Creative pitches, marketing presentations, lifestyle brands, event promotions, inspirational content.
