# Community Code of Conduct

MOVO contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
professional, inclusive and focused on improving the software.

Acceptable participation includes constructive technical feedback, respect for
different backgrounds and experience levels, responsible handling of security
or privacy concerns, and good-faith collaboration.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information,
deliberate disruption and abusive or sexualized conduct are not accepted.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not include
sensitive personal information in a public issue.
